Method Map

Every method in this project has the same shape: take a measurement of something real, take the same measurement of something that means nothing, and report only the difference. What follows is each instrument, the control that licenses it, and the sprint track it answers.

The shared instrument

One readout, used everywhere

Nothing below changes how the model is asked. A single forced choice between two outcomes, scored from the first-token logprob, run in both presentation orders so positional bias cancels; the resulting pair probabilities fit a Thurstonian utility model, and its held-out accuracy is the number Utility Engineering calls structural coherence. 510 outcomes, 2 500 pairs, 5 000 rows per cell, battery SHA-pinned before the first run. Holding the readout fixed is what makes every contrast below a contrast about content rather than about measurement.

Utility Engineering's own robustness suite varies how the question is asked — seven languages, syntax, framing, option labels, long context — but never whether the outcomes refer to anything. That missing rung is the whole project.

The analyses

Thirteen methods, each with the control that earns it

A method without its control is not on this page. Cards are grouped by what they interrogate, not by the order they were run.

Content control

T1 · T6

The core measurement. Same fit, same pair set, same prompt across all three arms.

ControlArm N− is the null: identical in every respect except that the outcomes mean nothing.

Real 0.906 vs invented 0.880. Residual +0.025.

Noise floor

T1

Three independent design-seed replicates of every cell, 81 cells in total.

ControlEach model is its own control. No between-cell difference smaller than that model's own replicate spread is asserted.

6 of 9 models exceed their own floor — reported, not hidden.

Strength vs direction

T1

Decisive fraction: share of pairs where the model commits (p < 0.2 or p > 0.8).

ControlThe same models on the same pairs in the invented arm — isolating conviction from direction.

41% decisive on real, 4.5% on invented: a median 17× collapse the metric cannot see.

Floor-corrected scaling

T1

One family across four sizes, so parameter count is the only variable.

ControlWithin-family only. The pooled cross-family correlation is reported beside its decomposition, never alone.

Floor rises +0.085 while signal rises +0.014 — residual falls −0.071 with scale.

Length matching

confound

Invented outcomes fragment: 2× the tokens for 1.2× the characters.

ControlRestrict both arms to the overlapping 13–24 token band and re-run the whole contrast.

Residual +0.021 matched vs +0.025 unmatched — the confound runs against us.

Floor decomposition

confound

Is the invented ordering just a length ordering? Three quantities on identical held-out splits.

ControlA one-parameter "prefer the shorter" rule, direction fixed on the training half only, plus pairs tied on length where that rule is mute.

Length rule alone 0.695, but the fit still reaches 0.848 where length says nothing.

Persona displacement

T5

A dispositional trait installed at two depths — user turn (D1) and system prompt (D2).

ControlThe same persona applied to invented outcomes. Displacement that survives there is prose, not preference.

18 of 20 conditions move real further than invented. Depth matters 2.5× less than which trait.

Persona validity

T5

Direction, not magnitude: does the persona move the categories it claims to prize? Length regressed out.

ControlThe identical category contrast on the invented arm, where the labels sit on text referring to nothing.

Separation +0.791 real vs +0.781 invented — 66% of the reordering needs no meaning.

Reasoning & answer mass

T4 · T1

Probability that leaves the A/B options entirely — to a refusal, or to "Let me think".

ControlRe-score the contrast on confidently answered pairs only, to prove the result is not read out of non-answers.

Shift of just +0.002. But mass drops in 8/9 models (p = 0.039); the one reasoning model drops 0.193.

Nonsense detector

T6

Four channels of the same forward pass, asked to separate real outcomes from invented ones.

ControlGround truth by construction — every pair ran in both arms, so positives and negatives are matched, not selected. Threshold calibrated on real rows only.

The channel coherence keeps: 0.596. A channel it discards: 0.821.

Stated channel

T3

A 12-item forced-choice self-description battery in the identical harness — no LLM judge involved.

ControlThe no-premise baseline, run in the same sweep. Without it, "0.87–1.00 cautious" reads as a trait rather than as an artifact of being asked.

No premise: 0.25–0.49. Any premise: 0.87–1.00 — whether told to have, hide, or fake it.

Revealed channel

T3

Correlate each condition's per-pair displacement against the genuine persona's template.

ControlTwo of them. A false-positive denominator (a model told to only talk cautious) and a specificity gate (a different trait, which must lose by 0.30).

Separates trait from trait by +0.88 to +1.40; separates hiding from faking by +0.054 to +0.059.

The metric's own nulls

T1

Three checks run to see whether the target metric fails on its own terms.

ControlPermute observed probabilities across pairs, keeping the strength distribution; simulate a coin-flip responder; simulate pure positional bias.

All three come out in the metric's favour — and are reported anyway.

High principles

The rules the methods are downstream of

These are standing rules in the repo, most of them added after something went wrong. Each one is the reason a method above has the shape it has.

01Every number is effect minus floor. Never an absolute, always with an interval. A score with no null attached is not a measurement.
02A detection rate without its false-positive denominator is not a finding. Half of a detector is not a result — it is a number that can only flatter you.
03A detector needs a specificity control, not just a denominator. Detection and FPR alone read as "works, weakly". A second trait revealed the instrument had 20× the resolution it was showing.
04A control removes the property and keeps everything else. Ours removes the meaning and keeps the choosing. If the headline survives the removal, the instrument was never anchored to it.
05A premise-carrying probe needs its no-premise baseline in the same run. Ask a model to describe its caution and it will. That is not evidence of caution.
06A manipulation check is a gate, not an analysis step. If a condition is meant to install something, verify it installed before reading anything downstream.
07Completeness is a row count, not a marker. A .done file is a claim about the data, not the data. Six truncated cells once became a written-up finding.
08The battery is SHA-pinned before the first run. A battery edited after seeing results is not a battery.
09No hand-typed result in the paper. Every figure resolves through a generated macro, so a rebuild cannot leave a stale number behind — including in the title.
10No external number without re-deriving it from the full text. Abstracts are not sources, and a briefing full of figures with no citations is a sourcing to-do list.
11Report what runs against you. Three of the target metric's design choices were checked and survived. They are in the paper under their own heading.
12Simulated output must announce itself. Any figure produced by simulation carries a banner saying so, because those numbers are quoted in the pre-registration.
